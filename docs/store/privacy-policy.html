MetroMitra is a Delhi Metro helper app published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). It is built to work offline. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run advertising or analytics in this version.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The metro dataset (stations, lines, routes) that comes with the app.
  • Your saved places, recent searches and settings, kept only on your phone.
  • A saved copy of the last app settings downloaded from our server (for example an announcement), so the app still works offline.

Location

Location is optional. The app asks for it only when you tap a location feature (nearby stations, live location on the map, journey mode). Your position is used on your phone to find the nearest station or to show your dot on the map. It is not saved, not sent to us and not shared. If you decline, every feature still works by choosing stations by hand. We do not track your location in the background.

Information sent over the internet

  • App settings. On start-up the app may download public settings (announcement text, feature switches, a few labels) from our server. This request carries no account, no identifier and no location. Like any web request, the server operator can see your IP address and the time of the request.
  • Dataset updates. If enabled, the app downloads signed metro data updates. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Online assistant (optional). If a question cannot be answered from the offline data, the app may send the text of that question to our assistant service to get an answer. We do not send your location, name or any identifier. The service does not store the question text. Answers are checked on your phone against the metro data, and the app shows which parts are verified.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no contacts, camera, microphone, phone or storage access.
  • No advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this version. If we add any later, we will update this policy and the Play Store data safety form first.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Accuracy of information

Fares, timings and station details come from public sources and may be incomplete or out of date. Where something is not verified, the app says so. Always follow the signs and announcements of the Delhi Metro Rail Corporation. MetroMitra is not affiliated with or endorsed by DMRC.

Children

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can deny or withdraw the location permission in Android settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
