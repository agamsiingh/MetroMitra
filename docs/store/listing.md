# Store listing (draft text)

**App name (30 max):** MetroMitra: Delhi Metro
**Short description (80 max):** Delhi Metro routes, fares and map that work offline. No sign-up.

**Full description (4000 max):**
MetroMitra helps you get around the Delhi Metro without confusion.

- Pick two stations and get the route, interchanges and number of stops.
- Works offline: the metro data is inside the app.
- Station search that understands English and Hindi names.
- Network map you can zoom, search and see your own position on (location is optional).
- Ask simple questions about stations and routes. Answers come from the app's own data, and anything not known is
  said to be unknown, never guessed.
- No account, no ads, no tracking.

Important: MetroMitra is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Delhi Metro Rail
Corporation (DMRC). Information can be incomplete or out of date; check station signs and official announcements.
