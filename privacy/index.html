MetroMitra – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

MetroMitra ("the app", "we") is a metro guide app for finding stations, routes, fares and nearby stations. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not. In short: the app has no account system, does not store personal data on our servers, and processes your location only on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No sign-up, login, or user profile.
  • No name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or camera access.
  • No background location tracking. Your location is never saved to a server or shared with anyone.

2. Location

If you use features such as "Nearby" or "Use my location", the app asks for location permission (approximate or precise, your choice). Your position is used on your device only to find the nearest stations or to match your journey. It is not logged, not stored, and not sent to our servers or any third party. You can deny the permission and still use the app with manual station selection. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

3. AI assistant questions

When the built-in assistant cannot answer from the offline metro data and you have online AI enabled, your question is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our AI proxy, together with the language setting and the relevant station facts from the app's dataset. Your location is never included. Our proxy does not log the text of your questions by default, and it keeps only the rate-limit counters needed to prevent abuse. The AI provider used to generate answers processes the request under its own terms. You can turn online AI off in Settings; the offline assistant keeps working without it.

4. Metro data updates

The app may download updated station, route and fare datasets over HTTPS. Each update is checked (digital signature, checksum and schema validation) before it is used. These requests do not include your location or any personal identifier.

5. Advertising

The free version may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier and related technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance, under Google's privacy policy. Where required by law, you will see a consent form (Google UMP) before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later under "Ads privacy" in the app's Settings. Where ads are turned off, no advertising identifier is used by the app.

6. Crash reports and diagnostics

The app may send anonymised crash logs (stack traces and device model/OS version) to help us fix bugs. These do not include your location or the text of your questions.

7. Data storage on your device

App settings (for example your language and assistant preferences) and the offline metro dataset are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

8. Third parties

Apart from the services described above (Google AdMob for ads, our AI proxy and its AI provider for optional AI answers, and the hosting used to deliver dataset updates), we do not sell, rent or share your information.

9. Children

MetroMitra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

10. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke location permission, disable online AI, manage ad consent, and uninstall the app at any time, which deletes all data stored on your device. Because we do not hold an account or personal profile for you, there is typically no server-side data to access or delete; if you have a specific request, contact us.

11. Security

Network communication uses HTTPS only. API keys are kept on our server and are not included in the app. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we design the app to keep the data it handles to a minimum.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing or in-app notice where appropriate.
